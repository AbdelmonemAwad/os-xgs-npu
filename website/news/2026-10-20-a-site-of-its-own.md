# A site of its own

The project has a domain of its own, **npuforge.org**, and this is its site: the documentation, the
measurements and the story in one place, searchable, in light and dark, with a home page in English
and [in Arabic](/ar/).

<!-- more -->

Nothing here is written twice. The pages are the repository's own, and each change to them reaches
the site the day it is merged; the photographs are the same ones, under the same licence.

What else moved to the domain:

- **Problem reports** from the appliances go to `reports.npuforge.org`, from release 0.1.0-alpha.2.2.
- **Mail** reaches the project at contact@npuforge.org, and a vulnerability at
  security@npuforge.org - though a private report on GitHub is still the first way.
- **The image builder** is at build.npuforge.org.

The site keeps no cookies and fetches nothing from anyone else; [privacy](../../website/pages/privacy.md)
says what it does keep.
