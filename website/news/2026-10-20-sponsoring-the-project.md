# Sponsoring the project

os-xgs-npu has a GitHub Sponsors page now: https://github.com/sponsors/AbdelmonemAwad

<!-- more -->

Until now everything on the bench has come out of my own pocket: the XGS 136 and the two XGS 3300s,
the measuring equipment and tools, the SFP modules and an expansion module, and every month the
Cloudflare services and the domains behind npuforge.org.

Sponsorship pays for what the work still needs: the other models of each family (the XGS 87, 107,
116 and 126 beside the 136; the XGS 2100, 2300 and 3100 beside the 3300), more of the expansion
modules, optics, a traffic generator that can load several 10G ports at once, and the running costs.

The one-time tiers follow real prices: $100 buys a used desktop XGS for the bench, $500 a used 1U
one. Monthly sponsorship keeps the site running and every release measured before it ships.

Everything stays open source under BSD-2-Clause, for sponsors and everyone else alike.

The same words are an announcement in the repository's
[Discussions](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/discussions/609).
