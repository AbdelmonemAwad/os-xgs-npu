# Forms

Every form the project's repositories have on GitHub, one link each. Nothing a form asks for is private: leave out addresses, MAC addresses and serial numbers. A problem on an appliance can also go from the appliance itself, with no GitHub account: **XGS: Problem Reports** files it, cleaned of all three.

## os-xgs-npu

The drivers, the XGS pages and the coprocessor.

| form | for |
|---|---|
| [Something does not work](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/issues/new?template=problem.yml) | A driver, a page, an update or a boot that does not do what it should. |
| [My appliance, and what works on it](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/issues/new?template=appliance.yml) | An XGS running os-xgs-npu - which model, and what works and what does not. It becomes a row of the [compatibility list](/compatibility/). |
| [A measurement or a hardware finding](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/issues/new?template=measurement.yml) | A figure you took, or something about a board, a module or a chip that the pages do not say. |
| [An idea or a request](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/issues/new?template=idea.yml) | Something the drivers or the pages could do, or another appliance they could support. |
| [A pull request](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/compare) | A change. Its template asks for the appliance, the version and the commit that ran - [how one is reviewed](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#how-a-pull-request-is-reviewed). |
| [A security vulnerability](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/security/advisories/new) | Privately, never in an issue; [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/blob/main/SECURITY.md) says what to include. |
| [A question](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu/discussions) | How to do something, or whether an appliance could work. |

## Arabic

A plugin's pull request may come without the Arabic of its page strings, from a contributor who does not write it: the string shows in English until its Arabic is added in opnsense-arabic. [Strings waiting for Arabic](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/opnsense-arabic/issues?q=is%3Aissue%20in%3Atitle%20%22Strings%20waiting%20for%20Arabic%22) lists every one still waiting, and [a wrong or missing translation](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/opnsense-arabic/issues/new?template=translation.yml) is the form for one that reads wrong.

Anything else: [write to the project](/contact/).
