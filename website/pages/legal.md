## What was read, and what is not here

The protocol the drivers speak was recovered from Marvell's GPL-2.0-only sources, published by Sophos
in its SFOS_OSS release, and from the binaries the appliance ships. **No vendor code is included or
redistributed.** Facts - offsets, command numbers, field widths - are transcribed; expression is not
copied. [Where the knowledge came from](../../docs/provenance.md) sets out what was read, from where,
which sentences are quoted and why, and what is deliberately absent.
