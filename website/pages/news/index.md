# News

here is announced before it is done. Follow it with any feed reader at
[news/feed.xml](/news/feed.xml), or on GitHub with **Watch** - **Custom** - **Releases** on
[os-xgs-npu](https://github.com/AbdelmonemAwad/os-xgs-npu).
