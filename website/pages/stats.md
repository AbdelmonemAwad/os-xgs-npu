# Visits

What this site and the project are used for, counted by the site itself: the pages read in the
last thirty days and which were read most, the appliances that count themselves, and the images
built. A page read is its address and the day, and nothing about who read it - no cookie, no
address, no browser - and a browser that asks not to be followed is not counted at all.
[Privacy](privacy.md) says the rest.
