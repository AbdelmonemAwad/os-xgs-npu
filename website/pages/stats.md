# Visits

How many pages of this site were read in the last thirty days, by day, and which were read most.
The site counts them itself: a page's address and the day, and nothing about who read it - no
cookie, no address, no browser - and a browser that asks not to be followed is not counted at all.
[Privacy](privacy.md) says the rest.
